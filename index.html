Privacy Policy

This application does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All settings and preferences are stored locally on your device only.

No data is sent to any external servers. There are no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services integrated in this app.

The app requires certain system permissions (Display over other apps, Accessibility Service, Battery optimization exemption, Foreground service, and Wake lock) solely for its core auto-tapping functionality. These permissions are not used to collect any information about you or your device usage.

Upon uninstalling the app, all locally stored data is permanently deleted.

Permissions Used

Display Over Other Apps

Needed to show the floating overlay circle on top of other applications. Without this permission, the overlay cannot be displayed.

Accessibility Service

Used to dispatch automated tap gestures at the configured position. This permission is essential for the auto-click feature to work. You can disable this service at any time from your device's accessibility settings.

Battery Optimization

Allows the app to run smoothly in the background. Without this exemption, aggressive battery optimization on certain devices (Xiaomi, Samsung, Vivo, Oppo, etc.) may stop the service.

Foreground Service

Keeps the auto-click loop running in the background. A persistent notification is displayed while the service is active.

Wake Lock

Prevents the CPU from sleeping when the screen is off, ensuring the tap loop continues to run reliably.

Post Notifications (Android 13+)

Required to display the foreground service notification on Android 13 and above.
