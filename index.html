Privacy Policy

1. Data we collect

Auto Clicker does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. Your settings (tap interval, overlay size and position) are saved only on your device. No data is sent to any server, and there are no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking services in this app.

2. Accessibility Service (AccessibilityService API)

Auto Clicker uses the Android AccessibilityService API only to perform tap gestures at the screen position you choose with the floating circle, at the interval you set, after you press Start.

The service does not read, record, store or share what is shown on your screen, including text, passwords, messages and notifications. It is not used for advertising, analytics or any purpose other than performing taps.

Before you are taken to Accessibility Settings, the app shows a prominent in-app disclosure and you must tap Accept. The service stays off until you enable it, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings > Accessibility.

3. Other permissions

  • Display over other apps: shows the floating circle on top of other apps.
  • Foreground service and Notifications: keep the tap loop running and show its status notification.
  • Wake lock: keeps the CPU awake while the tap loop runs.
  • Battery optimization exemption: stops the system from closing the app in the background.
  • Autostart (Xiaomi / Redmi / Poco only): lets the app keep running in the background on MIUI / HyperOS.

None of these permissions is used to collect information about you or your device.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell, share or disclose any data to third parties.

5. Data deletion

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all locally stored data.

Permissions Used

Display Over Other Apps

Needed to show the floating overlay circle on top of other applications. Without this permission, the overlay cannot be displayed.

Accessibility Service

Used only to perform tap gestures at the position you configured. It does not read or collect your screen content or personal data. Before you are taken to Accessibility Settings, a dialog explains this and you must tap Accept or Decline. You can disable the service at any time in your device's accessibility settings.

Battery Optimization

Allows the app to run smoothly in the background. Without this exemption, aggressive battery optimization on certain devices (Xiaomi, Samsung, Vivo, Oppo, etc.) may stop the service.

MIUI Autostart (Xiaomi/Redmi/Poco only)

Xiaomi, Redmi and Poco devices have an additional Autostart setting that must be enabled for the app to run in the background. Without it, the system may kill the service even if Battery Optimization is exempted. A dialog explains this and you can choose Allow or Not now.

Foreground Service

Keeps the auto-click loop running in the background. A persistent notification is displayed while the service is active.

Wake Lock

Prevents the CPU from sleeping when the screen is off, ensuring the tap loop continues to run reliably.

Post Notifications (Android 13+)

Required to display the foreground service notification on Android 13 and above.
